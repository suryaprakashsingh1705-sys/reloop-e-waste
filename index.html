QUICK GUIDE

Before it leaves your hands.

✳

A few safer habits can protect people and keep useful materials in circulation.

01
Back up & sign out

Save your data, sign out of accounts, and remove SIM or memory cards where possible.

02
Keep batteries intact

Don't puncture, crush, or dismantle batteries. If swollen or damaged, avoid handling and seek specialist advice.

03
Use a suitable channel

Choose an authorized e-waste collection or recycling route. Don't put electronics in regular household bins.

ⓘ

Guidance only. Follow local rules and the recycler's instructions for your specific item.
